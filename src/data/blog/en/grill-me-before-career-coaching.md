I have been in career coaching for four years now, working regularly with a fantastic coach. Just this morning, we had one of the most valuable, productive conversations in all four years.

What made this session so effective came down to three distinct stages: before, during, and after. The biggest difference maker was what happened beforehand—I had an AI thoroughly grill my recent progress and mindset before I ever got on the call.

## Before the Session: Uncovering Real Shifts with Grill-me

My preparation routine for this session looked like this:

First, I pulled up my notes from our previous coaching session as a baseline and took stock of everything that happened over the past two months.

Next, I used the [Grill-me SKILL](/blog/posts/en/client-scope-explosion-two-skills) to have an AI read both the previous notes and the recent updates. Its job was to press on the details the raw notes left out, focusing on two things:
1. Real events: Specific turning points rather than abstract conclusions.
2. Emotional shifts: What doubts, hesitations, or unexpected feelings came up as events unfolded.

After being grilled into a corner with nowhere to hide, all the scattered thoughts condensed into a tight cheat sheet covering just three topics. Each topic had to include three clear components:
- Core tension: What the central conflict or dilemma actually is.
- Supporting facts: Objective events that ground the tension.
- Working hypothesis: My current take or tentative solution.

## During the Session: 80/20 Guidance to Target Blind Spots

A great coach does not push their own agenda; they guide with precision.

Throughout the call, I spoke for about 80% of the time, while my coach spoke for only 20%.

Yet every intervention in that 20% cut right through to my blind spots. In this session, a few key realizations struck home:
- "Mission" and "identity" can be decoupled.
- A "second career" is not a necessity.
- Past unrelated experience is not necessarily a liability.

That high-leverage guidance—without imposing viewpoints—felt like working with a human version of a top-tier AI assistant. It does not rush to give you answers. It holds your chaos, asks a few pointed questions, and forces you to face the blind spots you could not navigate around on your own.

## After the Session: Turning Conversation Notes into Next Steps

The moment the call ended, I handed all the notes I took during the conversation to an AI assistant and asked it to organize the takeaways into a clear, actionable task list.

From grilling out the core issues before the call, to letting the coach dismantle my blind spots during it, to locking down actionable steps immediately afterward—running this full loop cleared away the mental fog, leaving a straight path to keep moving forward.

Technical details and configurations used in this workflow:
